1.1 Who We Are

As technology evolves, so does the relationship between the public sector and the people we serve. GovTech Consulting (GTC) was established to advance Singapore’s national mandate on building a resilient digital foundation for our public infrastructure.

To meet the complex demands of an AI-driven world, the GTC Design team partners with agencies to share tools, grow skills, and cultivate lasting digital capabilities. As government agencies are central to driving sustainable digital growth, we work side by side to empower public officers in fulfilling their core mission — transforming services and elevating the quality of tech for the public good, together.
