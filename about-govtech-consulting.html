1.1 Who We Are

GovTech Consulting (GTC) was established in June 2025 to advance Singapore’s national mandate for a resilient digital public infrastructure, supporting agencies to navigate the complex, shifting demands of an AI-driven world.

As government agencies drive the digital future of public services, the GTC Design team partners directly with agency counterparts to rethink how their services are discovered, designed, and built — adapting digital experiences as the public’s relationship with government evolves. Now fifty strong and steadily growing, our team brings together diverse backgrounds to introduce novel ways of meeting complex challenges. Working side by side with public officers, we collaborate to fulfil a shared mission: transforming services and elevating the quality of technology for the public good, together.
