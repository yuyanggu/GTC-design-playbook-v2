Foreword

Welcome to the GTC Design Team playbook — a shared space to align on our principles, craft, and how we work. Because design never stands still, neither do we. This remains a living draft, constantly refined as we adapt to new challenges and evolve our methods together.

For GTC Designers

This is not a manual for design mechanics, research techniques, or workshop instructions. Instead, it captures how we work, our point of view on where our craft is heading, and what it means to do this work in government, for the public, right now. Treat this playbook as a reference — somewhere to revisit, question, and build from as we keep learning.

For Friends of Design

Our playbook is open because no one should have to figure this out alone. As technology transforms our craft and we continue to experiment, we know shared learning is what helps the wider tech community move forward together. Whether you are a fellow designer, a partner, or just curious about how we work, this space exists to spark new conversations and shape our collective thinking as we build this future together.
